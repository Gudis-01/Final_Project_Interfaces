# AI Usage

**AI level:** Level 2 (Guided Mode), requested from the professor before the submission date. No AI was used for the commercial script as part of this file's scope.

## Tools used

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Claude (Anthropic) | Brainstorming, help with the Unity + Vosk integration, planning the interface|


## What it was used for

- **Ideation:** After the professor said the first idea was too easy, we discussed extensions: speaker separation, ambient-sound alerts, accessibility settings.
- **Code assistance:** Questions and suggestions about connecting Vosk transcription to Unity and choosing a model size for Quest performance.

## How we checked and modified the output

- We ran everything on a real Meta Quest 2 instead of trusting generated code. For example, we tested the large Vosk model, found it too slow, and decided to keep the small one.
- We changed the suggested interface to match our own sketch and the minimalist style we wanted.
- User research and testing data come only from our real sessions; the AI provided table structures and question templates, not results.

## Authorship

All team members reviewed and tested the AI-assisted work and can explain it in the individual defense.
